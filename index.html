Good to know.

Are the products genuine?

Yes. We sell original branded products only, and every purchase is billed with GST.

Is there a warranty?

Yes, on footwear. Genuine branded footwear comes with a 1-year warranty. The warranty does not cover clothing. Ask us in the store for the details on your pair.

Do you sell online?

No. urbané is a walk-in store, so you can see, touch and try everything before you buy. Call or WhatsApp us with a question, then come visit.

Do you clean and repair shoes?

Yes. Premium shoe care is exclusive to urbané and hard to find at other shoe stores. Drop your pair at the store and ask Aman.
